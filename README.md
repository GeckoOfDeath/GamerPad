# GamerPad 👾🕹️
GamerPad is a keyboard design that would allow you to quick chat in games without having to use time consuming text strings.

🔥🔥🔥Description🔥🔥🔥

I worked around 20 hours on the project (missed time on lapis LoL). Even though i had to take a big break i think it came out well. I have given it a KWK firmware but small adjustments may be needed after receiving the parts. It will type say "/ hello" on its own so you can avoid all the random "enter" and "/" while gaming!

## Features:
- White PLA case!
- 128x32 OLED Display
- EC11 Rotary encoder for switching modes
- 12 WS2812B RGB LEDs. For Maximum underglow.
- 3 Keys

## CAD Model: 
It friction fits with 2 solid pieces. It has gaps for airflow along with optimal underglow experience without ypur hand falling into it. It uses a organic design to keep a natural atheistic. It can fit into your palm with ease along with being erginomic.

## Firmware Overview
This hackpad uses KMK firmware for everything. 

- the rotary encoder acts as a button. Will change your "mode" in the future.
- The 3 keys currently act as macros.
- The OLED is untested (due to lack of oled) But has been set up!
